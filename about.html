Our Purpose

Technology that makes caring for loved ones a little easier.

CareX connects families with local people who can provide elderly care, companionship and hospital assistance.

The Challenge & The Solution

Why CareX?

Caring for aging family members shouldn't depend on luck, distance, or informal networks.

The Problem

Modern urban families face difficult logistical realities when balancing careers and senior health:

Geographic Distance

Children frequently live and work far away in distant metros, unable to reach parents immediately during daytime appointments.

Hospital Navigation Stress

Elderly parents struggle alone through crowded OPD queues, diagnostic test labs, and confusing billing counters at major hospitals like AIIMS.

Lack of Vetted Reliability

Finding background-checked, polite, and trustworthy assistance on flexible hourly or daily terms has traditionally been difficult.

The CareX Solution

CareX brings both sides together through one dignified, transparent digital marketplace:

For Families Seeking Care

Seniors and their family organizers find vetted, respectful neighborhood companions with transparent rates, real-time ETA, and doctor notes.

Connected Neighborhood Community
For Verified Local Companions

Educated youth, nursing graduates, and compassionate residents turn spare hours into dignified micro-employment with 100% direct payouts.

Shared Community

Two sides. One simple platform.

Designed with balanced clarity for both families looking for care and caregivers sharing their time.

For Families

Find local care providers and companions when you need support.

Whether it's an urgent 10:30 AM appointment at AIIMS, daily medicine reminders, or warm conversation over afternoon tea, families can book verified support without friction or hidden agency fees.
