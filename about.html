Our Purpose

Technology that makes caring for loved ones a little easier.

CareX connects families with local people who can provide elderly care, companionship and hospital assistance.

The Challenge

Why CareX?

Caring for aging family members shouldn't depend on luck or informal networks.

The Problem

Modern families face difficult logistical realities when balancing careers and senior health:

  • Distance: Children frequently live far away in different cities or busy work districts.
  • Hospital Navigation: Elderly parents often struggle to reach diagnostic appointments and hospital check-ins alone.
  • Reliability: Finding trustworthy, background-checked local assistance on short notice is notoriously difficult.

The CareX Solution

CareX brings the two sides together through one dignified, transparent digital marketplace:
